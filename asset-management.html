Overview

Every asset, accounted for

Asset Management is the module of the Raster hospital management suite that keeps a register of the institution's equipment and infrastructure — biomedical devices, IT hardware, furniture and fixtures — with each item's location, custodian, purchase details and service history.

Maintenance schedules, calibration due dates and warranty periods are tracked against every asset so that nothing lapses unnoticed, and depreciation and current value are available to the finance team from the same record. Because it shares the iHMS platform, purchase and store transactions flow into the register without re-entry.
