Overview

The heart of the imaging enterprise

If a hospital is to thrive in the healthcare marketplace of the future, it has to put a premium on IT investment. Image archives and data storage are increasingly a “must-have”. While putting fully operational electronic health records and PACS enterprise archives in place remains the top priority for the hospital, adopting a corresponding strategy for managing these systems is an issue that has to be addressed. Images make up a critical part of the diagnostic process, and a comprehensive image exchange reduces administrative workload and enables faster treatment.

The data storage system is the heart of a PACS. A reliable, high-capacity store that provides immediate access to the entire imaging archive with minimal operator intervention forms the foundation of every PACS installation.

With Raster iPACS, radiologists complete procedures faster, saving time through rapid image access and high-performance loading. iPACS features a variety of applications to master a broad range of clinical challenges, and can be tailored to suit personal preferences.

It is easy to service, with low implementation effort and high remote serviceability. The modular, scalable design opens up new possibilities for integration and future growth — protecting your investment and saving cost by reusing existing infrastructure. Deployed alongside other Raster products and any imaging hardware, you benefit from a single-vendor solution of the highest quality.

How iPACS fits together

The PACS / RIS server sits at the centre: modalities send studies in, workstations and mobile devices read them, and archives, printers, disc burners and remote sites connect around it — on site and over the internet through a firewall.
