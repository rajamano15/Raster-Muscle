Welcome to Raster Images

Engineering the continuum of care

Raster Images Private Limited is an Information Services & Technology Company with over 30 years of professional experience, providing software consultancy and solutions to cover the entire continuum of patient care and hospital administration to clients globally. We are involved in several verticals, of which healthcare is our primary focus.

Dr. Suresh Viswanathan, a medical doctor with long-standing interests in medical informatics and imaging, started the software development company named Raster Images to pioneer medical imaging in the region. Today Raster Images has matured into a recognised organisation in the IT industry for designing, developing, implementing and training highly technological and innovative solutions for complex problems faced by both the private and public sector.

Through market leadership, financial strength and responsible business practices, we demonstrate our enduring capacity to serve any business as key partners. Raster Images has been a “Trusted Adviser” for many medical institutes.

History

Part of the SKS Group

Based out of Salem, Tamil Nadu and started in 1987, the SKS Group has interests in Automotive, Healthcare, Information Technology & Education, with over 1200 employees. Our core focus on customer centricity, good business practice, employee welfare and sustainability has held us in good stead as we move into our fourth decade of operations.

1987

SKS Group

Founded in Salem, Tamil Nadu, growing across Automotive, Healthcare, Information Technology and Education to over 1200 employees today.

1987

SKS Hospital

Salem's oldest multi-speciality hospital. Established with 85 beds, today it has over 250, offering primary, secondary and tertiary care across all specialities. A super-speciality referral centre for the region with several pioneering firsts — the first renal transplant and cardiac surgery in the region, and pioneering endourology and laparoscopy surgery in India.

1993

VS Educational Trust

Formed by the founders of SKS Hospital as a non-profit organisation to provide quality nursing education. VSET runs the SKS School of Nursing on the hospital premises, and its graduates are in demand wherever they go.

2005

SKS Automobiles

Started as a 3S dealership in Salem, now with branches at Hosur, Krishnagiri, Dharmapuri, Namakkal, Tiruchengode and Attur — covering sales, service, body and paint work, and spares for the Mahindra passenger and commercial range.
