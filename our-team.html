Founder

Where it started

Dr. Suresh Viswanathan, a medical doctor with long-standing interests in medical informatics and imaging, started the software development company named Raster Images to pioneer medical imaging in the region.

That clinical grounding still shapes how the company works: the people building the software understand the department it is going into. Today Raster Images is a recognised organisation in the IT industry for designing, developing, implementing and training highly technological and innovative solutions for complex problems faced by both the private and public sector.
